# Community code of conduct

Motion Shell welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project. Critique designs and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately exclude contributors. Avoid publishing private information, credentials, or security-sensitive reports in public issues.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when behavior harms the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
